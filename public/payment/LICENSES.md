# Payment marks

- visa.svg, mastercard.svg, amex.svg: from the `payment-icons` npm package (MPL-2.0, https://github.com/activemerchant/payment_icons).
- applepay.svg, googlepay.svg: marks from `simple-icons` (CC0-1.0), placed on a card background.

The marks belong to their owners and are shown only to say which payment methods checkout accepts.
